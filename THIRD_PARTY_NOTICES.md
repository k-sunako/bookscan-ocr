- [OCRmyPDF-PaddleOCR plugin](https://github.com/clefru/ocrmypdf-paddleocr) — check the current repository license and release.
- [PaddleOCR](https://github.com/PaddlePaddle/PaddleOCR) — Apache-2.0 code; review the license/terms associated with the particular model weights.
- [Tesseract OCR](https://github.com/tesseract-ocr/tesseract) and its separately distributed language data.

Keep downloaded model weights and language data out of this repository unless their redistribution terms have been explicitly checked.
